# Example: Board Presentation on Digital Transformation

## Executive Summary

We recommend investing €15M in digital transformation to increase revenue by 25% and reduce costs by €8M annually within 24 months.

---

## Storyline

### Opening (SCQA)

1. **[Situation]** Our industry is shifting from physical to digital at 30% CAGR
2. **[Complication]** However, 80% of our revenue still comes from legacy channels facing 5% annual decline
3. **[Question]** How do we transition to digital while protecting existing revenue streams?
4. **[Answer]** We recommend a €15M digital transformation across 3 pillars to capture €20M new revenue

### Supporting Points

5. **[Point 1]** Pillar 1: Launch mobile-first platform to capture younger demographic (€12M revenue)
6. **[Point 1 Detail]** Mobile users represent 65% of target market but only 15% of our customers
7. **[Point 2]** Pillar 2: Digitize core operations to reduce costs by €8M annually
8. **[Point 2 Detail]** Automation will eliminate 40% of manual processes while improving accuracy
9. **[Point 3]** Pillar 3: Build data analytics capability to personalize customer experience
10. **[Point 3 Detail]** Personalization drives 30% higher conversion based on industry benchmarks

### Synthesis

11. **[Financial Impact]** Combined impact: €20M additional revenue and €8M cost savings by Year 2
12. **[Implementation]** 24-month rollout in 3 phases starting Q2 2024
13. **[Risk Mitigation]** Phased approach allows us to test and learn while protecting base business
14. **[Recommendation]** Approve €15M investment to begin Phase 1 execution in Q2

---

## Detailed Slide Content

### Slide 1: Our industry is shifting from physical to digital at 30% CAGR

**Supporting Evidence:**
- Digital channel revenue grew from €50B (2020) to €120B (2024) = 30% CAGR
- Physical channel revenue declined from €200B to €160B = -5% CAGR
- By 2027, digital will represent 60% of total market

**Visual:** Dual-axis chart showing digital growth vs. physical decline
**Source:** Industry Association Report 2024, McKinsey Digital Transformation Study

---

### Slide 2: However, 80% of our revenue still comes from legacy channels facing 5% annual decline

**Supporting Evidence:**
- Current revenue split: 80% physical (€160M), 20% digital (€40M)
- Physical revenue declining 5% annually
- At current trajectory, we lose €40M revenue by 2027
- Digital-native competitors gaining 15 market share points

**Visual:** Waterfall chart showing revenue erosion over 3 years
**Source:** Internal finance data, competitive analysis

---

### Slide 3: How do we transition to digital while protecting existing revenue streams?

**Key Considerations:**
- Cannot abandon €160M physical business overnight
- Must grow digital fast enough to offset physical decline
- Need to avoid cannibalization during transition
- Competitors are 3 years ahead in digital maturity

**Visual:** Simple text slide with question prominently displayed
**Source:** Strategic planning workshop

---

### Slide 4: We recommend a €15M digital transformation across 3 pillars to capture €20M new revenue

**Overview:**
- **Pillar 1:** Mobile platform (€8M investment, €12M revenue)
- **Pillar 2:** Operations digitization (€4M investment, €8M cost savings)
- **Pillar 3:** Data analytics (€3M investment, 30% conversion uplift)
- **Total:** €15M investment, €28M impact (€20M revenue + €8M savings)

**Visual:** Three-pillar diagram with investment and return for each
**Source:** Business case analysis

---

### Slide 5: Pillar 1: Launch mobile-first platform to capture younger demographic (€12M revenue)

**Business Case:**
- Target: 18-35 age group, 2M potential customers
- Conversion: 3% (60K customers)
- Avg revenue: €200/customer/year
- New revenue: €12M annually by Year 2

**Visual:** Funnel showing market → target → conversion → revenue
**Source:** Market sizing analysis, conversion benchmarks

---

### Slide 6: Mobile users represent 65% of target market but only 15% of our customers

**Gap Analysis:**
- 65% of target market prefers mobile-first experience
- Our current mobile offering: basic website, no app
- Mobile users in our base: only 15%
- Opportunity: 50 percentage point gap represents €12M revenue

**Visual:** Bar chart comparing market preference vs. our penetration
**Source:** Customer survey (n=1,000), web analytics

---

### Slide 7: Pillar 2: Digitize core operations to reduce costs by €8M annually

**Cost Reduction Breakdown:**
- Process automation: €4M (eliminate manual data entry)
- Supplier portal: €2M (reduce procurement overhead)
- Self-service platform: €2M (reduce call center volume 40%)
- Investment: €4M, Payback: 6 months

**Visual:** Waterfall chart showing cost reduction by initiative
**Source:** Process analysis, vendor quotes

---

### Slide 8: Automation will eliminate 40% of manual processes while improving accuracy

**Impact Analysis:**
- Current state: 15 FTEs on manual data entry, 5% error rate
- Future state: 9 FTEs with RPA, 0.5% error rate
- Productivity gain: 40% capacity freed for value-add work
- Quality gain: 90% reduction in errors

**Visual:** Before/after comparison diagram
**Source:** Process mapping workshops, RPA vendor assessment

---

### Slide 9: Pillar 3: Build data analytics capability to personalize customer experience

**Personalization Strategy:**
- Collect: Customer behavior, preferences, purchase history
- Analyze: ML models to predict next-best-action
- Act: Personalized offers, recommendations, content
- Result: 30% increase in conversion rate

**Visual:** Data flywheel diagram
**Source:** Industry benchmarks (Amazon, Netflix case studies)

---

### Slide 10: Personalization drives 30% higher conversion based on industry benchmarks

**Evidence:**
- Amazon: Personalization drives 35% of revenue
- Netflix: Personalized recommendations → 80% watch time
- Retail average: 20-30% uplift in conversion
- Our baseline: 2% conversion, Target: 2.6% (+30% relative)

**Visual:** Bar chart comparing personalized vs. non-personalized conversion
**Source:** McKinsey personalization report, industry case studies

---

### Slide 11: Combined impact: €20M additional revenue and €8M cost savings by Year 2

**Financial Summary:**
| Initiative | Investment | Year 1 Impact | Year 2 Impact |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Mobile platform | €8M | €4M | €12M |
| Operations digitization | €4M | €6M | €8M |
| Data analytics | €3M | €2M | €8M |
| **Total** | **€15M** | **€12M** | **€28M** |

**Net Impact:** €13M positive by end of Year 2

**Visual:** Stacked bar chart showing investment vs. returns over time
**Source:** Financial model

---

### Slide 12: 24-month rollout in 3 phases starting Q2 2024

**Implementation Timeline:**
- **Phase 1 (Q2-Q3 2024):** Foundation - infrastructure, team, pilots
- **Phase 2 (Q4 2024-Q2 2025):** Build - mobile app, automation, analytics platform
- **Phase 3 (Q3 2025-Q1 2026):** Scale - full rollout, optimization, expansion

**Key Milestones:**
- Q3 2024: Mobile app beta launch
- Q1 2025: Operations automation live
- Q2 2025: Full platform launch
- Q1 2026: €28M annual impact achieved

**Visual:** Gantt chart with phases and milestones
**Source:** Program plan

---

### Slide 13: Phased approach allows us to test and learn while protecting base business

**Risk Mitigation:**
1. **Cannibalization risk:** Run mobile as incremental channel (new segments only) in Year 1
2. **Execution risk:** Hire experienced digital leader, use proven vendors
3. **Technology risk:** Use proven platforms (not bleeding edge)
4. **Change management risk:** Dedicate 20% of budget to training and communication

**Go/No-Go Gates:**
- Phase 1 → Phase 2: Achieve 10K beta users, 3% conversion
- Phase 2 → Phase 3: €4M revenue, €2M cost savings achieved

**Visual:** Risk matrix with mitigation strategies
**Source:** Risk assessment workshop

---

### Slide 14: Approve €15M investment to begin Phase 1 execution in Q2

**Recommendation:**
We recommend the Board approve:
1. €15M capital allocation for digital transformation
2. Authorization to hire Chief Digital Officer
3. Q2 2024 start for Phase 1 execution

**Expected Returns:**
- €28M annual impact by Year 2
- 87% IRR over 3 years
- Strategic positioning for digital-first future

**Next Steps:**
- Finalize vendor selection (March 2024)
- Recruit CDO (April 2024)
- Begin Phase 1 (May 2024)

**Visual:** Simple text slide with clear asks
**Source:** Executive recommendation

## Key Takeaways from This Example

1. **Storyline tells complete story** - You can read just the titles and understand the full recommendation
2. **Action-oriented titles** - Every title is a sentence that communicates insight
3. **Data-backed** - Every claim supported by specific numbers and sources
4. **SCQA structure** - Clear situation → complication → question → answer flow
5. **Rule of 3** - Three pillars, three phases, three bullets per slide
6. **Executive-ready** - Decision is clear, risks addressed, next steps defined

# Storyline Template

## SCQA Framework

**Situation (Context):**
- Slide 1: [Current state - what is true today]

**Complication (Problem/Opportunity):**
- Slide 2: [What changed or what problem emerged]
- Slide 3: [Why this matters / implications]

**Question (What needs to be answered):**
- Slide 4: [The key question the audience needs answered]

**Answer (Your recommendation):**
- Slide 5: [Your recommendation in one clear statement]

---

## Supporting Points (Rule of 3)

**Point 1:**
- Slide 6: [Main point 1 - action title]
- Slide 7: [Supporting detail for point 1]

**Point 2:**
- Slide 8: [Main point 2 - action title]
- Slide 9: [Supporting detail for point 2]

**Point 3:**
- Slide 10: [Main point 3 - action title]
- Slide 11: [Supporting detail for point 3]

---

## Synthesis & Next Steps

**Financial/Operational Impact:**
- Slide 12: [Quantified impact of recommendation]

**Implementation:**
- Slide 13: [Timeline, resources, key milestones]

**Risk & Mitigation:**
- Slide 14: [Key risks and how you'll address them]

**Recommendation:**
- Slide 15: [Restate recommendation with clear call to action]

---

## Appendix (Supporting Analysis)

- Slide 16+: [Detailed methodology]
- Slide 17+: [Additional data]
- Slide 18+: [Alternative options considered]
- Slide 19+: [Assumptions and sources]

---

## Quality Checklist

**Elevator Test:**
- [ ] Reading only slide titles tells complete story
- [ ] No gaps in logic
- [ ] Flow is natural

**So What Test:**
- [ ] Every title answers "so what?"
- [ ] Implication is clear
- [ ] No label titles

**Executive Test:**
- [ ] C-level would understand without explanation
- [ ] Data supports every claim
- [ ] Decision is clear

**Simplicity Test:**
- [ ] One message per slide
- [ ] Max 3 bullets per slide
- [ ] No jargon
- [ ] Clear visuals
